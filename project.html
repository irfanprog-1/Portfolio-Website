The Creative Challenge

The client needed to translate complex SaaS capabilities into snappy, immediately understandable visuals without bogging the viewer down in dry UI recordings.

The Editing Strategy

We rebuilt core UI widgets into vector assets inside After Effects, added custom kinetic cursor paths, dynamic optical zooms, and a rich multi-layer foley soundtrack featuring mechanical keystrokes, subtle air risers, and sub-bass impacts.

Technical Execution

Utilized Adobe Premiere Pro for master timeline pacing, After Effects for 2.5D camera choreographies, and DaVinci Resolve for custom neon electric-blue highlights.
